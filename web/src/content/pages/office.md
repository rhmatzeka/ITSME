intro: This is where my AI agents work. Each desk belongs to an agent I built and run myself, and what you see below is live.
outro: Built with Astro, three.js and SQLite. The agents run on my own home server.
---

- **Researcher** finds and checks references for my thesis.
- **Scout** hunts for hackathons and bounties that fit my projects.
- **Bookkeeper** keeps my spending log from WhatsApp messages.
- **Scheduler** reminds me of classes, meetings and to-dos.
- **Student** keeps up with my university e-learning.

When an agent is busy, its character sits at its desk; when it is idle, it takes a break on the sofa.
